# Writing for EWU Express

EWU Express is the student news publication of East West University. This guide
is for student journalists, contributors, and editorial staff who want to pitch
or write stories for the platform.

## How to pitch a story

1. **Know the sections.** EWU Express publishes in four categories:
   - **Campus Heat** — breaking campus news, controversies, and timely stories
   - **Stories** — feature pieces, profiles, and human-interest reporting
   - **Events** — festival guides, orientation coverage, major campus events
   - **Did You Know?** — short, verified campus facts and useful student info

2. **Pitch before you write.** Send a short pitch to the editorial team with:
   - What the story is and why it matters to EWU students
   - Which section it fits
   - How you will report it (who you will talk to, what you will verify)
   - A rough word count

3. **Report it properly.** Verify claims with more than one source where
   possible. Name your sources unless they have a credible reason to stay
   anonymous, and tell the editor why anonymity is needed. Do not publish
   rumours as facts.

## Writing the story

- **Lead with the hook.** The first paragraph should tell the reader why they
  should care *now*.
- **Use real voices.** Quotes from students, faculty, and staff make stories
  live. Get permission before you attribute anything sensitive.
- **Keep it readable.** Short paragraphs. One idea per paragraph. Avoid jargon
  that only you understand.
- **Fact-check everything.** Dates, names, titles, numbers, discount claims —
  verify before you submit. A single wrong name undermines the whole piece.
- **Photos matter.** A strong cover image makes a story get read. Use your own
  photos where possible; if you use someone else's, get permission and credit
  them.

## Submissions and editorial process

- Submit drafts through the admin dashboard or directly to the editorial team.
- The editorial team assigns each story a category and may edit for length,
  clarity, and accuracy.
- Stories can be published without a category and assigned later from the admin
  panel — so do not wait for a category decision to submit.
- Once published, stories are emailed to subscribers via the newsletter. Write
  with that audience in mind.

## Ethics

- Be fair. If someone is criticised, give them a chance to respond before
  publication.
- Be accurate. Correct errors promptly when they are brought to your attention.
- Be transparent. If you have a conflict of interest relevant to a story,
  tell the editor before you write it.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information that is not in the
  public interest.

## Questions

Direct questions to the editorial team through the admin dashboard or the
repository owner.
